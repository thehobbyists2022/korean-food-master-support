Privacy Policy & Food Safety Terms

Korean Food Master (K-Food Master) · Last updated: September 2026

Summary: "K-Food Master" is a private-first, offline culinary companion app. We DO NOT collect personal information. Every recipe, favorite, language setting, shopping preference, dietary filter, and cooker selection is stored 100% locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Account Required: You can explore all recipes, street market guides, pantry tips, timers, and clerk cards without creating an account or logging in.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not ask for, harvest, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or camera feed.
  • No Financial or Payment Data Storage: We do not process payments or credit card information.

2. Local Storage & Device Preferences

To ensure our offline-first experience and personalised recipes, Korean Food Master stores the following data locally on your device using localStorage:

  • Design & Culinary Language: Chosen display language (English, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Thai) so you can cook in the interface of your choice.
  • Selected Cooking Units: Metric (g / ml) or US (oz / cup).
  • Audio sound effects: Timer sound settings and mute preference.
  • Saved cookware multipliers and step preferences.

You can always leave, reset, or clear this data on your end through your browser or device storage settings.

3. Affiliate & Third-Party Retailer Links

Korean Food Master contains links to help you locate Asian grocery or authentic ingredients like gochujang, gochugaru, soybean paste, and so on. If you choose to purchase using these links, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Amazon Associates Program: As an Amazon Associate, Korean Food Master earns from qualifying purchases. When you click Amazon ingredient links marked as sponsored, we may earn a commission. Participating retailers include Amazon.com and other official marketplace stores.

4. Food Safety & Cooking Disclaimer

The recipes provided in Korean Food Master are intended for general food preparation and educational purposes only.

  • Appliance Settings Vary: Air fryer, Instant Pot (pressure cooker), and microwave settings differ by brand, model, and wattage. Always follow the manufacturer's manual for liquid ratios, venting, and pressure-release procedures. Start with the shorter cooking time and adjust as needed.
  • Hot Oil & Spatter Caution: Always exercise extreme caution when heating or deep-frying oil. Keep a safe distance, use appropriate tools, and keep children and pets away from the cooking area.
  • Nutrition Disclaimer: Calorie and nutrition values shown in this app are approximations provided for convenience. Do not use them as a replacement for professional medical or dietary advice. Always consult a licensed nutritionist or physician for specific dietary, medical, or health-related questions. By using the recipes in this app, you agree to do so at your own risk; Korean Food Master and Clarity Clinical Solutions LLC accept no liability for injury, illness, or loss arising from improper food storage, handling, or preparation.
  • Dietary Modifications: Some recipes may need to be adapted for allergies, gluten intolerance (celiac disease), or vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, low-sodium, nut-free, or low-sugar lifestyles. Recipe ingredients, substitutions, and dietary guidance may need to be adjusted to those needs.
  • Guidance & Adjustments: Korean food flavors, pot styles, ingredient times, and safety precautions vary across regions and households. Temperatures, times, and quantities are always provided as a starting point and adjusted based on your judgment, brand of appliance, skill, and local conditions.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. It is designed for general audiences, culinary exploration, and healthy home cooking.
