Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Korean Food Master works fully without any sign-up. All of your preferences, favorites and notes are stored only on your device.

Does it collect my data?

No. We do not collect personal data and we do not track your location. See our Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All recipes and the lab are built into the app, so you can cook and browse without a network connection.

Which languages are supported?

English, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Japanese and Thai — switch anytime from the language menu.

Some recipe ingredients are hard to find. What now?

Every recipe includes a smart substitution guide, and the Pantry Kit gives sourcing tips plus a card you can show to supermarket staff.
